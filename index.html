How it works

  1. Choose your apps

    Select the distracting apps that take up your time. BibleFirst helps you keep them out of reach using Apple's Screen Time.

  2. Read God's Word

    Open BibleFirst and read your daily Bible passage. No quizzes or complicated routines.

  3. Get back to your day

    Once you've finished reading, your selected apps unlock so you can carry on with your day.

Build a habit that matters

  • A fresh Bible passage every day, plus a verse of the day.
  • Highlight meaningful verses in blue or yellow, and share them.
  • Explore Bible translations in English, Spanish and Portuguese.
  • Your reading and selected app choices stay on your device.
