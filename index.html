How it works

  1. Pick your apps

    Choose the apps that eat your time. BibleFirst locks them using Apple’s Screen Time.

  2. Read today’s passage

    Open BibleFirst and read a short passage. No timers, no quiz.

  3. Your apps unlock

    Once you have read, your apps are yours for the rest of the day.

Made for a daily habit

  • A new passage every day, plus a verse of the day
  • Highlight verses in blue or yellow, or share them
  • Several translations, in English, Spanish and Portuguese
  • Your reading and your app choices stay on your device
