Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What BibleFirst collects

BibleFirst does not collect, store on a server, or share your personal information. It has no accounts.

What stays on your device

Your reading position, highlights and settings are saved only on your device. The apps you choose to lock are handled by Apple’s Screen Time framework. Apple does not give BibleFirst a readable list of your apps, and nothing about your selection leaves your device.

Third parties

BibleFirst does not include advertising or tracking.

Children

BibleFirst does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
