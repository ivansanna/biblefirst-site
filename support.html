Support

Need help with BibleFirst? Email support@trybiblefirst.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version. I reply as soon as I can.

My apps did not unlock

Open BibleFirst, finish today’s reading, then wait a few seconds. If they stay locked, make sure Screen Time access is still allowed for BibleFirst in Settings, then restart the app.

I want to change which apps are locked

Open the app settings in BibleFirst and choose your apps again.

I want to delete my data

Everything is stored on your device, so deleting the app removes it.
